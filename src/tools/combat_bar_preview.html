Armed, waiting for the second click

Dodge, Dash, Disengage and the bonus actions spend the turn the instant they land and there is no undo in a fight, so they arm first. The state has to read as a CHANGE while the pointer is still sitting on the slot — that is why it adds a ring rather than only warming the border the way hover does. Every bar on this page is live: click one and it arms, click again and it clears, or wait three seconds and it forgets.
