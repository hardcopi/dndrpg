# SUNDERMERE
### A Continent of the Sundered West — Gazetteer, Volume One

Nine realms. 245 settlements. 46 landmarks. Just under two million souls, spread across roughly 1,850,000 square kilometres of forest, fell, mere and burning south.

This volume covers the shape of the world and who claims what. Cities get their own volumes as we build them.

---

## 1. The Shape of the World

Sundermere is not one landmass but two, joined by a thread.

The **northern continent** runs east to west for some 1,400 miles: a long spine of mountains along its top edge, a great freshwater inland sea below that, and a broken southern coast facing open water. The **southern subcontinent** is warmer, drier, flatter, and larger than it looks. Between them lies **the Sundering** — a gulf that very nearly cut the world in half and stopped, leaving a single narrow isthmus.

Whoever holds that isthmus holds every overland trade route between north and south. It is currently held by a committee of merchants who cannot agree on lunch.

### The Waters

| Water | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Sundering** | The great gulf. Sixty miles of open water at its narrowest, and every ship that crosses it pays Thrail. |
| **The Ythan Mere** | Inland freshwater sea in the north. Grain, iron, and the entire economy of the Ythan League float on it. |
| **The Cold Gyre** | The northwestern ocean. Ice nine months a year. Hjaldmark's longships come out of it. |
| **The Bale Sea** | The eastern ocean. Warm, deep, and badly charted past the hundred-fathom line. |
| **The Wracks** | The southwestern waters. Named for what washes up. |
| **The Dragon's Bight** | A wide northeastern bay under the shadow of Karrun-Deep. Nobody remembers which dragon. |

### The Heights

**The Sceptres** run across the north-centre — sharp, young, snow-locked above four thousand feet, and the reason Hjaldmark and Drennmark have never successfully invaded each other.

**The Kalder Wall** continues that spine into the northeast and is the roof over Karrun-Deep. The dwarves did not build under the Wall because it was convenient. They built there because of what the Wall is made of.

**The Thornspine** runs north-to-south down the eastern side, dividing Sylvarien's vales from the coastal plain. Only one reliable crossing: **Hanged Man's High Pass**, which is exactly as welcoming as it sounds.

### The Green and the Dry

The northern continent is forest — **the Wyrdpines** in Drennmark's cold interior, **the Elderwood** across Ardennia's heart, **Gravebriar** and **Duskhollow** in the disputed middles, and **the Hush** on Ordrane's northern border, which is called that for the obvious reason.

The south is another matter. Three named deserts — **the Ashen Reach**, **the Sunfields**, and **the Thirst** — cover most of Sarathal and a third of Ordrane. They were not always deserts. There is good agreement on that and no agreement at all on why they became deserts.

## 2. The Nine Realms

### ARDENNIA — *The Ardent Marches*
**Capital** Vantry (136,000) · **Population** 322,900 · **32 settlements** · Western north-continent

> The last unbroken fragment of the Ardent Empire. Its armies are still the finest on the continent and its treasury is empty.

Ruled by **King Aldric IV**, aged eleven, through **Lord Regent Sevrin Maur**, who is competent, unloved, and running out of time. Humans, with old-blood halfling farm clans through the downs.

Ardennia is the continent's past pretending to be its present. Every institution here was designed to administer an empire three times this size, and they all still run — courts, roads, the standing legions, the imperial post — at a cost the realm cannot possibly meet. Maur has been selling imperial charters to Thrail merchants for six years. When Aldric comes of age he will inherit a kingdom that has already been sold in pieces.

**Cities** Gwenhallow · Marlworth · Morworth · Vanmarch · Caeholt

---

### THE YTHAN LEAGUE — *The Free League of the Ythan Mere*
**Capital** Lathmere (74,300) · **Population** 184,600 · **23 settlements** · The inland sea

> Controls every barge on the inland sea. Its charter is older than any crown and it enforces it with grain prices.

Ruled by **Halvia Corr, Mistress of the Mere**. Humans and river-folk, plus a large and very well-funded gnomish artificer quarter in Lathmere.

The League has no army worth the name and has never needed one. It has the Mere, and the Mere feeds the north. In the famine of forty years ago the League set a price, held it, and three kingdoms did as they were told. Nobody has forgotten this — least of all the League, which has grown quietly certain it could do it again.

**Cities** Waerhaven · Torgild · Skelmere · Lathreach

---

### SYLVARIEN — *The Vale-Realm*
**Capital** Aelthyr (137,300) · **Population** 268,900 · **24 settlements** · Eastern vales, below the Thornspine

> Sylvarien remembers the cataclysm that made the Sundering. It has never told anyone what it was.

Ruled by a hereditary council of nine vale-houses under **Coronal Ithren Vaelas**, now in the eleventh century of her reign. Elves, half-elves, and the wood-gnomes of the lower vales.

Sylvarien is the oldest continuous polity in the world and behaves like it — slow, courteous, and entirely unhurried by anyone else's emergency. It is also the only realm whose records predate the Sundering, and those records are not in the library. They are in Aelthyr, below it, in a room that four people may enter.

Vaelas has recently begun receiving Ordranian ambassadors personally. She has not done that in two hundred years.

**Cities** Ithwyn · Vaelien · Saelien

---

### KARRUN-DEEP — *The Hold-Kingdoms*
**Capital** Zharrund (83,000) · **Population** 127,300 · **21 settlements** · The Kalder Wall, northeast

> Three of the nine holds have gone silent. The Deepmoot has voted, six to nothing, not to discuss it.

A confederation of nine delving holds, each sovereign below ground, meeting as **the Deepmoot** under the Nine Thanes. Dwarves, with human surface-tenants in the valley towns.

What you see on the map is the smaller half of Karrun-Deep. Zharrund's surface town sits in a green vale; the hold itself runs eleven miles into the Wall and down further than the survey charts admit. The valley towns — Durgate, Anggforge, Bargorm, Vunrund, Durvault, Kazbrand, Tormvault — are gates and markets, not settlements in their own right.

Six votes were cast on whether to discuss the silence. Three Thanes did not vote, because three Thanes were not there.

---

### DRENNMARK — *The Old Kingdom*
**Capital** Ashen Drenn (58,500) · **Population** 84,300 · **15 settlements** · Northern taiga

> Something in the Wyrdpines has been moving south for six years. The Wardens have stopped reporting it.

**King Torvald the Sleepless** holds a crown; the **Grey Wardens of the pine-marches** hold everything else. Humans of the northern forest, with lycanthropic bloodlines scattered through the old families and not much fuss made about it.

The poorest and emptiest of the nine, and the only one with a functioning early-warning system for the far north — which is why the other realms tolerate the Wardens' near-total independence. Torvald has not slept a full night in nine years. He is not being poetic about this and neither is his physician.

**Cities** Stenwold

---

### HJALDMARK — *The Clanlands*
**Capital** Vorrsgard (51,700) · **Population** 96,400 · **16 settlements** · Northwestern coast

> Hjaldmark longships have started raiding Ardennia again. Not for gold. They are looking for something.

Clan-holds bound by oath to **High Jarl Sigrunn Ash-Hand**, oaths renewed each midwinter and by no means always renewed. Humans of the frozen coasts; goliath clans in the high fells.

Sixty years of quiet trade ended two summers ago. The raids are small, precise, and aimed at monasteries, archives and old shrines — never at treasuries. Ardennia has read this as barbarian confusion. Sylvarien has read it correctly, and said nothing.

**Cities** Fjorness

---

### THRAIL — *The Free Cities*
**Capital** Thrailgate (131,100) · **Population** 248,700 · **25 settlements** · The isthmus

> Whoever holds the isthmus holds the continent. Thrail holds it by owing everyone money.

A merchant concord of seven chartered cities under **the Seven Syndics**, elected annually and bribed continuously. No standing army. Everyone lives here; Thrail asks no one's blood, only their tariff.

The most valuable ground in the world, held by the least defensible state in it. Thrail's survival strategy is elegant and completely deliberate: it is in debt to every realm on the continent, and each of them would rather protect the debtor than let a rival collect. It works beautifully right up until two creditors agree.

Ordrane and Ardennia have begun to agree.

**Cities** Serrcross · Quillstead

---

### ORDRANE — *The Kingdom of Ordrane*
**Capital** Corrowmarch (134,200) · **Population** 317,800 · **40 settlements** · The southeast

> The rising power. Ordrane has spent thirty years buying what Ardennia inherited, and is nearly finished.

A centralised kingdom under **Queen Maerwyn the Younger**, with a genuinely dangerous knightly order, **the Iron Wake**. Humans, plus a large half-orc population through the border marches who have fought in every Ordranian war and are still arguing about the terms.

The largest realm by settlements and the only one whose institutions were designed for the size it currently is. Maerwyn is thirty-four, has been queen for eleven years, and has not lost anything yet. Her grandfather started buying Ardennian charters; her father bought Thrail debt; she is buying Sylvarien's attention, which is the expensive one.

**Cities** Stanstead · Blackmarch · Halbarrow · Standown · Brackmoor

---

### SARATHAL — *The Sun-Throne*
**Capital** Zir Sarath (141,400) · **Population** 171,300 · **10 settlements** · The deep south

> Sarathal's god has been dead a thousand years. Two years ago, its temples began answering prayers again.

A divine monarchy under **Sunfather Amrahd IX**, who claims descent from a dead god. Humans of the deep south, and the dragonborn caste of the **Ember Legion**.

Ten settlements — the fewest of any realm — and the second-largest city in the world. Sarathal is Zir Sarath and its dependencies, an enormous river-fed capital ringed by three deserts that were fertile once. The theology has been comfortably empty for forty generations: the Sunfather's line ruled by inheritance and everyone understood the god was a formality.

Then the temples started working. Amrahd IX did not ask for this and does not appear to be enjoying it.

**Cities** Saradan

## 3. The Unclaimed Lands

Thirty-nine settlements and fourteen major landmarks lie outside any realm's writ — roughly a tenth of the habitable continent, concentrated in the deep interior, the far islands, and the mountain margins.

The names tell you what the settlers thought of the place: **Gravecairn**, **Bonereach**, **Wolfhollow**, **Mirefen**, **Crowfen**, **Widowhearth**, **Hunterfall**, **Sorrowwold**, **Ruincairn**, **Thornmark**.

These are the adventuring zones. No lord's justice, no garrison, no tax collector, and no help. Every one of them is on the map with a working population, a terrain type, and a road (or no road) connecting it to somewhere civilised.

---

## 4. Roads, Rivers and Reach

**213 routes** connect the 95 major settlements, and the network is fully connected overland — you can walk from Vorrsgard on the Cold Gyre to Zir Sarath in the deep south without once crossing water, provided you go through Thrailgate. Everyone does. That is the whole of Thrail's foreign policy.

- **24 highways** — realm-to-realm trunk roads, maintained (theoretically)
- **189 roads** — local links between towns

The outlying islands have no road at all. Villages there are reached by boat or not at all.

**70 rivers** are traced from source to sea; sixteen are named. The **Ythan** feeds the Mere. The **Corrow Water** runs through Ordrane's capital. The **Sarath Flow** is the only reason 141,000 people can live in a desert.

## 5. What's Actually Happening

Six threads, all live, all crossing:

1. **Ardennia is insolvent** and Regent Maur is selling the empire's bones to cover the interest. The boy-king turns twelve next spring.
2. **Ordrane is ready** and has spent three decades getting there. It needs a pretext, not a cause.
3. **Thrail's debt shield is failing** now that two of its creditors have started talking to each other directly.
4. **Hjaldmark is searching** the north coast for something, and burning archives to find it.
5. **Three holds of Karrun-Deep have gone silent** under the Kalder Wall, and the Deepmoot has formally resolved not to look.
6. **Sarathal's dead god is answering**, and the only realm that might know why is Sylvarien, which has been quiet for a thousand years and just started taking meetings.

Sylvarien sits at the centre of four of the six. That is not a coincidence, and Coronal Vaelas knows it.

## 6. Files

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `sundermere-map.svg` | The map. Vector, 2520×1764, scales to any size. |
| `sundermere-map.png` | 3200px raster, for anywhere SVG won't go. |
| `sundermere-explorer.html` | Interactive: zoom, pan, click any settlement or landmark, filter by realm. Open in a browser. |
| `sundermere-world.json` | All world data. Coordinates match the SVG viewBox exactly. |
| `sundermere-terrain.png` | 420×294 lookup image: R = biome id, G = elevation, B = realm index (255 = unclaimed). Sample this for terrain queries. |

### world.json structure

```
meta        width, height, grid [420,294], cellMi 4.04, seaLevel
realms[]    id, name, capital, ruler, government, peoples, colour,
            hook, population, area_km2, centroid, biomes{}
settlements[] id, name, tier, realm, x, y, pop, biome, localBiome,
            port, river, elev
road[]     id, a, b, grade (highway|road|searoute), len_mi, pts[]
rivers[]    id, name, flow, pts[], widths[]
features[]  kind (range|forest|desert|lake|sea|gulf|ocean|bay), name, x, y
landmarks[] id, name, type, x, y, biome, realm, danger (1-10)
coastlines[] closed polygons, evenodd fill — use for land/sea hit tests
```

All `x, y` are in map pixels against the SVG's `viewBox="0 0 2520 1764"`. One grid cell is 6 px is about 4 miles.
